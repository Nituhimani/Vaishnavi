Our Story

A shop built on real handloom, real prices.

Vaishnavi Handloom sits in the lanes of Badbazar, Kustapada, a short walk from the Samaleswari Temple — one of Sambalpur's oldest weaving neighbourhoods. Every saree is sourced directly, so customers pay for the weave, not the markup.

Suman Meher shares new arrivals and weaving stories every week on YouTube, where the shop has built a following of everyday saree lovers across India.
